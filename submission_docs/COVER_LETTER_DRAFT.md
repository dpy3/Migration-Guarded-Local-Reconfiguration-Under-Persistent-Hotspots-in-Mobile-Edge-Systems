Dear Editors of Pervasive and Mobile Computing,

Please consider the manuscript entitled "Migration-Guarded Local Reconfiguration Under Persistent Hotspots in Mobile Edge Systems" for publication in Pervasive and Mobile Computing.

The manuscript studies a narrowly defined online reconfiguration problem in mobile edge systems: how to preserve useful placement state during a persistent local compute, communication, or coupled hotspot while explicitly controlling local-search migrations. The proposed BudgetedLNS method starts from the repaired previous placement, localizes search to pressured nodes, uses a candidate pool bounded by the move budget, and applies lexicographic acceptance. Its implementation rejects repaired local trials above the budget and records any excess caused by the final feasibility repair.

The evaluation uses 30 paired seeds, 200 slots, and 180 persistent tasks for each of three hotspot mechanisms. At the frozen 5% operating point, BudgetedLNS improves satisfaction and reduces migrations relative to slot-wise FullResched and the implemented HIVE comparator. The manuscript reports exact paired intervals, a Holm-adjusted sign-flip test, runtime scope, and the failure boundary at a 1% budget. It also limits its claims to the implemented simulator, tested task scale, hotspot profiles, and budget settings.

The submission includes an Elsevier LaTeX source, vector figures, source-data CSV files, analysis code, and a checksum-tracked reproducibility package.
